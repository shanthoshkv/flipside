# Privacy policy

Flipside does not collect, transmit or share any data.

- **Storage**: tasks, sessions, plans, notes, flashcards (including images you add), settings and exams are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.
- **Network**: the app declares no internet permission and makes no network requests.
- **Accounts and ads**: none. No analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party SDKs that report data.
- **Sensors**: the accelerometer is read on the device only to detect a face-down phone. Readings are not stored.
- **Notifications**: used for timer alerts and the reminders you turn on. They are scheduled locally.
- **Do Not Disturb access**: optional. Used only to silence notifications during a focus session and restore your previous setting afterwards.
- **Photos**: only images you choose for a flashcard are copied into the app's private storage.
- **Backups and exports**: written only where you choose (a folder or the share sheet). You control those files.
- **Deleting data**: Settings → Reset all data, or uninstall the app.

Contact: open an issue in the project repository.
